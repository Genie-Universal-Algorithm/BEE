Abstract

Vision-language-action (VLA) models handle long-horizon manipulation, yet success hinges on a few precision-critical phases where millimeter-scale errors undo all prior progress. Online reinforcement learning can optimize exactly these actions, but free exploration is far too costly on real robots, which makes human corrections indispensable. Existing methods either cannot use those corrections or fold them into undifferentiated supervision. Human corrections are not uniformly noisy: they are reliable along some action dimensions and variable along others.

BEE treats a correction as evidence about a constraint, not as an action to copy. A Correction Model predicts how a human would correct a frozen VLA proposal, and how consistent that correction is on each action dimension. Consistent dimensions stay tight. Variable ones relax, so return can move the policy beyond expert imitation. On three real-world tasks and one LIBERO-PRO simulation task, at a matched online-data budget, BEE reaches the highest success rate on every task: 91.2% on average, against 57.5% for RLT and 42.1% for DSRL, with the lowest human intervention rate on all real-world tasks.

Method

The VLA stays frozen and still proposes the action chunk. BEE keeps that proposal, predicts where the human correction can be trusted, and trains only a lightweight residual.

  1. 01 Keep the proposal

    An extractor turns the frozen VLA into a latent token. With robot state, that token is the RL state. The action chunk remains an explicit proposal.

  2. 02 Model the correction

    On a human takeover, the Correction Model predicts the residual relative to the proposal and a variance on each action dimension.

  3. 03 Optimize the residual

    The learned residual is added to the proposal. The update trades off Q-value, staying near the VLA, and a state-dependent multiplier on the dimension-wise constraint.

Tasks

Three real-robot tasks end in a contact that a millimeter can undo. Bowl placing is the matched LIBERO-PRO task. Phone charging and cloth aligning are scored on the precision-critical phase. Snack hanging and bowl placing are scored on the full task.
